# 1. Overall Design Direction

Create a modern, professional, trustworthy automotive spare-parts
website for **SSS Auto Spares**, based in Chennai, Tamil Nadu.

The visual identity should communicate:

-   Genuine and verified automotive spare parts
-   Fast customer enquiry response
-   Reliable vehicle-part matching
-   Professional sourcing and inspection
-   Chennai-focused local service
-   Clear communication and trustworthy business operations

### Design Style

-   Modern B2B automotive service website
-   Clean white and very light blue backgrounds
-   Strong royal blue as the primary action color
-   Dark navy typography
-   Rounded cards and buttons
-   Subtle borders and soft shadows
-   Spacious layout with a premium corporate appearance
-   Clear hierarchy and highly readable content
-   Professional but approachable tone
-   Responsive on desktop, tablet, and mobile

### Suggested Visual Tokens

-   Primary blue: `#1749D1` to `#2454DC`
-   Dark navy: `#0B1B35` or `#10213D`
-   Light blue background: `#F2F6FF`
-   Pale blue surface: `#E5EEFF`
-   Text gray: `#526784`
-   Border: `#E4EAF3`
-   White: `#FFFFFF`
-   Accent orange for urgency/status: `#F5A623`
-   Success badge: soft green or pale blue

Use the exact colors consistently across buttons, icons, badges, links,
and highlights.
